About HubCity Preps

Eat well. Skip the work.

HubCity Preps is a locally owned Lubbock meal-prep company creating delicious, thoughtfully prepared food for people who want to eat well without spending their week planning, shopping, cooking and cleaning.

Our menus rotate between healthy comfort-food favorites, fresh nutrient-rich meals, globally inspired dishes, satisfying salads, flavorful bowls and easy grab-and-go breakfasts and snacks.

Everything is prepared with intention -- because eating healthier shouldn't mean giving up delicious food.

Whether you are a busy Lubbock professional, a student, a parent, or Texas Tech family sending a little taste of home to your student, HubCity Preps makes eating well simple.

You choose the food. We do the work. You enjoy the meal.
